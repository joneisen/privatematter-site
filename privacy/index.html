Private Matter

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026. Published by Iron Beard Digital, LLC. Contact: support@privatematter.app.

Summary. Private Matter keeps your journal on your iPhone. There is no account, no company server, no advertising, and no analytics. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your journal or anything about how you use the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter: entries, notes, locations you type, photos you add, illness episodes, and your settings. It is stored inside the app on your iPhone. We cannot see it, and the app has no way to send it to us.

Your notes, the place names you type, illness episode names and notes, and your photos are also encrypted inside the app, with a key kept in your iPhone's Keychain. Dates, times, and the options you choose (such as consistency type and symptoms) are stored without that extra encryption so the app can sort and summarize them; iOS still protects them along with the rest of your iPhone's data.

Photos and camera

Photos you take or choose are stored inside the app and are never saved to your photo library. When you choose from your library, the app receives only the photos you select. Each photo is re-encoded when it is added, which removes its location and other embedded metadata.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn it on, Private Matter saves two of the symptoms you record, bloating and abdominal cramps, to Apple Health. These are the only two that have an Apple Health equivalent. Private Matter does not read any of your Health data. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared by us with anyone. You can turn this off at any time in the app (Settings, Apple Health) or in the Health app.

Face ID, Touch ID, and passcode

App Lock uses Apple's system authentication. Private Matter only learns whether authentication succeeded. It never receives your face data, fingerprint, or passcode.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Their text does not mention what the app records.

Reports and exports

A PDF report or an export is created only when you ask for one, and you choose where it goes. Once you share it, how the recipient handles it is outside our control.

  • The readable JSON export is not encrypted.
  • The encrypted export is protected by a passphrase you choose. We never see the passphrase, and we cannot open the file without it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your iPhone.

Backups

iOS may include app data in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer. Those backups are controlled by you and your Apple Account settings. iCloud Backup and encrypted computer backups also carry the encryption key, so restoring from them brings your journal back in full. An unencrypted computer backup does not carry a key another iPhone can use, so notes, place names, episode names, and photos restored from it to a different iPhone cannot be opened.

Deleting your data

Settings, Privacy & Data, Delete all data removes your journal from your iPhone. It also removes the symptoms the app saved to Apple Health, where Health allows; anything Health does not let it remove stays in the Health app, where you can delete it. Deleting the app also removes your journal from your iPhone. Copies you exported or shared are not affected.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to answer you, and we do not add you to any list.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Private Matter is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.

Contact
