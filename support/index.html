Move your journal to a new iPhone

Your journal lives only on your iPhone, so it moves only when you move it.

  1. On your current iPhone, open Settings, Transfer & backup, and choose Create encrypted export. Pick a passphrase you will remember.
  2. Send the file to your new iPhone, for example with AirDrop.
  3. On the new iPhone, open Settings, Transfer & backup, Import journal, choose the file, and enter the passphrase.

Export your journal

  • Encrypted backup: Settings, Transfer & backup. This is the full record, protected by your passphrase.
  • Readable copy: Settings, Privacy & data, Export readable JSON. This file is not encrypted.
  • Report for your doctor: History or Insights, Report. Retrospective PDF reports are part of Private Matter Pro.

Delete everything

Settings, Privacy & data, Delete all data, then Delete everything. If App Lock is on, you will confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. This removes your journal from your iPhone and the symptoms it saved to Apple Health where Health allows. It cannot be undone, so consider an encrypted export first.

Subscriptions

  • Restore a purchase: Settings, Restore purchases, or Restore purchases on the Pro screen.
  • Cancel or change a plan: Settings, Manage subscription in the app, or the Settings app, your name, Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.
  • Refunds: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Apple Health

Settings, Apple Health. When you turn on symptom sharing, Private Matter saves bloating and abdominal cramps to Apple Health. It never reads your Health data. Turning sharing off withdraws the symptoms it saved.

App Lock

Settings, Privacy & data, App Lock. It uses your iPhone's own Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode, and you choose how soon it locks again after you leave the app.

"Your journal couldn't be opened"

This appears if your iPhone cannot open the journal, for example when storage is almost full. Nothing has been changed or deleted. Free up some storage and tap Try Again. If it keeps happening, email us and include the reference shown on that screen. Only choose Delete Journal and Start Over if you have an encrypted export or are sure you no longer need the journal.

Medical note

Private Matter is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose anything. If something worries you, talk to a clinician.
